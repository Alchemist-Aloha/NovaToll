Sources, coverage, and the supplied map

Operator estimates are retrieved from the Express Lanes calculator and VDOT’s I-66 inside-Beltway calculator. I-66 outside-Beltway planner estimates are requested from the visitor’s browser using the operator’s published gantry mapping. Missing component rates or blocked requests produce an incomplete total. Its planner describes historical-average estimates, so realtime accuracy is not verified. Connected-road trips combine current component estimates, which may change before reaching the second road. Fetch time is not proof of a new rate.

The supplied background is an illustrative, not-to-scale map. Dots are approximately calibrated to its drawn roads, not surveyed ramp locations. The image’s construction, study, and Opitz ramp labels are historical artwork; they are not current road-status information. Select and verify ramp names. Use the site while parked or have a passenger operate it.
